Our story

A Mersin street-food house, in Dalston.

Tantuni is the food of Mersin: fine lamb or chicken, parsley and tomato, folded into lavash and eaten hot from the grill. It is simple, generous and made for sharing.

We brought it to East London with the same charcoal, copper and hand-rolled bread. The menu travels from cold mezze and sizzling kavurma to çiğ köfte, künefe and a long table of rakı.
